## Canon flags on the script (not resolved here)
- Cut 10: the brand STOP block says no price in copy; the producer's line is "$99. The first 200."
  The preorder page the repo read shows $100 / $170. Needs a ruling before this card exists.
- Cut 06 line "already has the moment": the memory-trap rule says keep the verb on the day. The
  Pocket treatment used the same line (R/09 cut 07), so there is precedent; flagged, not changed.
- Cut 11: the film canon says never end on a logo card. Ending on him saying the name is fine;
  a logo after it is not.
- Cuts 03 and 08: the swipe gesture. The preorder page showed no cover, slide or button on the
  gold/blue tag (R/09 "Dylan's calls"). If the current product has no swipe surface, the honest
  "off" is the app mute or taking it off. Confirm against product truth before framing it.
