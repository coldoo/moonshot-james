# Moonshot — Launch Video Preview · Script v1 (producer, 2026-09-22)

Structure: presenter to camera, Dollar Shave Club walk-and-talk. Use cases happen around
him in the environment while he keeps talking. General-audience preview, not internal.

## Lines, in order

1. VO/presenter: "This is Moonshot. The best parts of your life happen away from a screen."
2. VO/presenter: "Moonshot sees and hears your day, so you can ask it anything about your life."
   (alt kept from producer: "It sees and hears your day, so it knows your life without you
   explaining any of it.")
3. Presenter: "One swipe and it's paying attention."
4. **[USE CASE A — simple, sexy, live]** The moment being captured. Nothing asked.
5. Presenter: "Later, I ask it anything,"
6. Presenter: "and Moonshot already has the moment."
7. **[USE CASE B — recall of the moment from A]** Producer's examples: the inverse steak
   (asks what went wrong last time) or the framed photo (asks what happened right after).
8. Presenter: "And when I want a moment to myself, one swipe. It stops listening."
9. On screen: "$99. The first 200. Ships [window]."
10. Presenter: "This is Moonshot."

## Decisions still open

- Which single use case fills A, and B must be its recall. Candidates from the previz:
  steak (A: flip question, B: what went wrong) or grandma at the piano (A: the moment,
  B: the photo on the wall). Steak is funnier and faster; grandma is warmer and shows the
  camera, not just the mic.
- Presenter: previz hero or new.
- Runtime target: this script reads at about 35–45 seconds spoken.
- Ships window text.
- Whether the swipe gesture on the pendant is shown in close-up at lines 3 and 8. It is
  the only product interaction in the piece and probably should be.
