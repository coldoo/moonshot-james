# Moonshot pendant

## What it is
Wearable pendant, worn at chest level. Captures continuously — ambient audio,
and one photo per minute. Runs all day without being triggered.

## Physical appearance (confirmed from reference images, 2026-09-19)
Rectangular with softly rounded corners, portrait orientation, roughly matchbox-face
size. NOT round. Two finishes: metallic gold and metallic blue. The lower two thirds of
the front has a raised flowing wave or ripple texture, like layered dunes. The upper
third is smooth, with a small round camera lens at the top left and a tiny dot (mic or
indicator) just to its right. A small metal bail at the top, with a thin contrasting
band, where the cord attaches. No visible screen. Hangs on a black braided cord.
Reference files: `assets/product_01_both_finishes.png` (both finishes, macro),
`assets/worn_01..03` (worn on people).

## How it sits on a body
Worn over clothing at upper chest, roughly sternum height, on the black braided cord.
Cord is thin, sits close to the neck, pendant hangs a hand's width below the collar.
Reads clearly over t-shirts, sweaters, and a shirt-and-apron.

## What has been agreed about the previz
Structure: three pairs plus one standalone. Each pair shows the same kind of
moment twice — once where no history is needed, once where the answer is only
possible because the device has been running for months.

Pair 1, follow-through. In the moment: two people talking, "I can help you
Saturday," captured. Context: before Saturday it surfaces the commitment,
draft ready.

Pair 2, practical. In the moment: cooking a steak for the first time. Context:
"How do I avoid overcooking this again?" — it saw the last attempt.

Pair 3, memory. In the moment: "Save Grandma's story about this song." Context:
looking at a photo on the wall, "What happened right after this?"

Standalone closer, pattern insight: an open life-optimising question. Leading
candidate "How do I best manage my time?" Final wording UNKNOWN: ask producer.

Output differs by query type: practical gets text plus an image, relational
gets a text summary, memory gets the answer plus one snapshot.

Rules agreed for use cases: the wearer always initiates, no monitoring of
other people, and the answer should need accumulated history rather than live
vision.

Staging note raised but not resolved: scenes with two people in frame risk
reading as surveillance of the other person. One proposed fix was staging the
wearer alone, with other people appearing only in recalled footage.
