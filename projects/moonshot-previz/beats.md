# Beats — Moonshot Present / Future

Every pair follows the same two-part shape so the audience learns the rule by scenario two:

**Present** — ordinary moment, pendant visible, something said or done that seems throwaway.
**Future** — time has passed, the moment matters again, pendant glows, help arrives.

| # | Scenario | Present | Future |
|---|----------|---------|--------|
| 0 | Cold open | Pendant on a collarbone, catches light | |
| 1 | Relationships | Two friends on a couch or at a cafe, casual chat, wearer says "yeah I'll help you move Saturday" | Friday evening, wearer alone at home or walking, pendant glows, glance at phone: the nudge |
| 2 | Cooking | Kitchen, steak in a pan, wearer leaning over asking how long, a little lost | Five months later, same kitchen, wearer holds up the pan with mock suspicion and asks how to not screw it up again, pendant glows |
| 3 | Memory | Living room, grandma at the piano, wearer sitting close, quiet, pendant glows very faintly: it noticed | Years later, wearer holding a printed photo of that afternoon, asks what happened right after, pendant glows |
| 4 | Pattern recognition | Rapid montage: commute, desk, gym, dinner, late night, all with pendant on. Weeks stacked up | Wearer on a balcony or quiet spot, asks what they should do more of, pendant glows, hold on their face taking in the answer |
| 5 | End card | Pendant macro, product name overlaid | |

Transitions: hard cuts between present and future within a pair. A distinct transition
(whip, flash, or dip) between pairs, chosen at assembly. The montage in scenario 4 is
several stills cut fast, no animation needed for most of them.
