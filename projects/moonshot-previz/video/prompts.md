## General rules, appended to every prompt

The man is the man from the reference in every frame and every angle; face stable, no
deformation. The product is the pendant from the reference, worn at upper chest, never
redesigned. Casual unpolished phone footage, handheld with slight natural shake, available
light, realistic skin, no beauty filter, not cinematic, not glossy, no dramatic color
grading. Diegetic sound only, no music. No on-screen text, no readable text on any screen.

## S01 — Street conversation · 8 s · start frame S01_v3

**Summary.** The friend asks for help on Saturday, the man says yes without thinking about
it, they keep talking, and the camera drifts in on the pendant on his chest. Nothing is
acknowledged. The pendant is simply there, listening.

**Timestamps.**
0–2.5s: The friend, in a light jacket, back three-quarter to camera, asks, "Hey, can you help me with this on Saturday?" A loose hand gesture toward the street. The man listens, weight on one foot, hands loose. Street ambience, distant traffic, a bird.
2.5–5s: The man nods once and says, "Yeah, I can help you Saturday." A small shrug with it, the way you agree to something you'll forget. Relaxed.
5–8s: They keep talking, the friend's reply indistinct under the ambience, the man half-laughs. The camera pushes in slowly and steadily toward the pendant on the man's chest until it fills the lower middle of frame, sharp, the conversation continuing above it. No glance at it from either of them.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: residential street, front steps and railing of a house, a tree, a parked car down the block, overcast daylight, as in the starting frame.

## S02 — Coming home · 8 s · start frame S02_v2

**Summary.** Days later. The man walks in through his front door, phone in hand but not
looking at it. The phone lights up. He glances down, reads, and quietly remembers he said
he'd help tomorrow. Small, not dramatic.

**Timestamps.**
0–2.5s: The man steps in through the front door, keys in one hand, phone held low in the other, not looking at it, eyes on the hallway ahead. Door creak, keys jingle, footsteps on wood.
2.5–4.5s: The phone screen lights up in his hand. He glances down at it. He slows, then stops.
4.5–8s: He reads for a beat, then a quiet exhale and a small nod to himself, half a smile, and says under his breath, "Oh yeah, I gotta follow up on that." He sets the keys on the side table without looking, eyes still on the phone. Quiet house, faint evening ambience through the open door.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: hallway with coat hooks, a small side table, a mat, the open front door behind him with evening light, as in the starting frame. He wears an open dark jacket over the t-shirt.

## S03 — The flip · 8 s · start frame S03_v2

**Summary.** Steak in the pan. The man leans over it with tongs, can't read the sear, asks
if it's ready to flip, waits a beat, flips it.

**Timestamps.**
0–2.5s: The man leans over the frying pan, tongs in hand, tilting his head to see the sear on the edge of the steak. He can't tell. Loud sizzle, steam rising.
2.5–4.5s: Without looking away from the pan he asks, "Is this ready to flip?" Plain, a little uncertain.
4.5–6s: He waits, tongs hovering over the pan. A small nod, as if he got an answer.
6–8s: He flips the steak in one clean motion. Fresh sear hiss and a spatter. He steps back half a step, satisfied. Sizzle settles.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: home kitchen, granite counter, white cabinets, stainless range and hood, warm overhead light, as in the starting frame.

## S04 — Weeks later · 8 s · start frame S04b_v4

**Summary.** Same kitchen, different day. Raw steak on the board, pan cold. The man looks at
it, we cut close on him, and he asks Moonshot what he did wrong last time.

**Timestamps.**
0–2.5s: Wide as the starting frame. The man stands a step back from the counter, hands empty at his sides, looking down at the raw steak on the board. He breathes in. Fridge hum, a clock.
2.5–3s: CUT to a close-up of the man's face and upper chest, the pendant in frame, same kitchen behind him, same daylight. He is still looking down at the steak.
3–6.5s: He says, evenly, "Moonshot, tell me what I did wrong last time so I don't overcook this steak again." Dry, half to himself.
6.5–8s: He waits, listening, eyes on the steak. A slow nod as the answer lands. He does not touch anything yet.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: the same kitchen as S03, daylight through the window, raw steak on a wooden board, clean cold pan on the hob, as in the starting frame.

## S05 — The piano · 6 s · start frame S05_v4

**Summary.** A memory being made. The man and his grandmother at the piano, having a good
time, their words indistinct. The pendant on his chest faces her the whole time. Nothing is
asked. This is the moment that S06 looks back on.

**Timestamps.**
0–2s: The grandmother, at the piano turned toward the room, is mid-story, one hand gesturing, the other on the keys. She presses two soft notes as she talks. The man stands a step away, hands at his sides, listening, the pendant on his chest pointed toward her. Her voice warm and indistinct, two piano notes, room tone.
2–4s: He says something short and they both laugh. She taps his arm, then plays a quick little run of notes to make a point.
4–6s: They keep talking, easy, both smiling. Soft afternoon light. The camera drifts very slightly, the way a phone does when nobody is holding it carefully. Two voices under the room, indistinct, the piano.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: warm living room, upright piano with framed photos on top, patterned curtains, soft afternoon light through them, a rug, as in the starting frame. The grandmother has grey hair and a dark top, as in the starting frame.

## S06 — The photo · 6 s · start frame S06_producer

**Summary.** Evening. The man alone in the hallway in front of the framed photo of that
afternoon. He is not confused, he is reminiscing. He asks Moonshot what happened right after
the photo was taken. The clip ends on him waiting, still looking at it.

**Timestamps.**
0–2s: The man stands still in front of the framed photograph, hands at his sides, looking at it. A small, warm exhale through the nose, the beginning of a smile. Quiet house, a clock.
2–4.5s: He says, softly and fondly, "Hey Moonshot, what happened right after this?" As he speaks his head turns a few degrees so the edge of his face shows in profile, the same man as the reference. The pendant cord is visible at his neck.
4.5–6s: He holds, waiting, eyes on the photo, the half-smile staying. Stillness.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: hallway, the framed photo of the grandmother at the piano on the wall, a lamp, warm evening light, as in the starting frame. The photo on the wall does not change. He does not turn fully to camera.

## S07 — The desk · 8 s · start frame S07b_v3

**Summary.** Late night at the desk, busy and worn down. The man stops, asks Moonshot where
he's wasting his time the most, and the piece ends on him with no answer shown.

**Timestamps.**
0–3s: The man sits at the desk, shoulders up, one hand flat on scattered papers, the other near his face, eyes on nothing. Behind him the window is dark with his reflection in it. He exhales slowly. Laptop fan, the lamp's faint hum, a distant car.
3–6s: Without changing position he says, tired and honest, "Hey Moonshot, where am I wasting my time the most?" A pause after it.
6–8s: He holds. His eyes stay on nothing. Nothing answers. The lamp hums. End on the stillness.

**General rules.** [as above]
Setting: home desk at night, laptop open, loose papers, a mug, desk lamp on, dark window behind him with the room reflected, as in the starting frame.
