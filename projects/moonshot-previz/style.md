## Proposed previz register (drafted here, not yet approved)

Given the brand voice ("Life, kept." plain, warm, domestic) the UGC realism register from
the one known prompt is a good fit for the present halves: ordinary light, phone-camera
honesty, nothing aspirational. The future halves can share it. Polish is not the point of a
previz anyway. Proposed prefix, to be replaced by the producer:

"Photorealistic candid photo, ordinary domestic setting, natural available light, realistic
skin texture and fabric, slight phone-camera imperfection, no beauty-filter skin, not
glossy, not cinematic, no dramatic color grading, no added text or graphic overlays."
